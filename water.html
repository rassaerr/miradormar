Emergency Procedure: In Case of a Water Leak

If you detect or suspect a water leak, immediately follow these instructions based on the location:

A) Leak INSIDE an Apartment

In the event of a leak inside an apartment, immediately isolate the water supply directly inside the property using the main internal stopcock (typically located inside the boiler cupboard; see Photo 6). If that valve is inaccessible, jammed, or does not resolve the leak, go to the main water cabinet at the entrance (see Photo 1), unlock the door using the key safe, and close your apartment's individual stopcock labelled 1A to 4D (see Photo 4).

B) Leak INSIDE the Building Common Area

In the event of a leak in the internal common areas of the building, go to the main water cabinet at the entrance and unlock the door using the key safe. Locate and close the stopcock labelled '14 LOCAL' to shut off the common area supply (see Photo 5).
Note: If the leak involves the swimming pool, close the unlabelled pool supply valve located at the bottom right of the cabinet (see Photo 7).

C) General Leak in the Building

In the event of a major or general leak in the building requiring the primary supply to be isolated, go to the main water cabinet at the entrance (see Photo 2) and unlock the door using the key safe. Completely close the main blue shut-off valve located at the bottom left of the cabinet (marked with a red circle in Photo 2; see also Photo 3).

D) Leak OUTSIDE the Building

If a water leak is observed on the street, pavement, or main supply line outside the building perimeter:

  • Do not attempt to operate internal valves. Immediately report the incident to the Galasa emergency line on +34 950 39 12 00 and notify building management.

Procedure in Case of a Main Water Shutdown (Water Cuts)

Due to the ageing main water supply infrastructure in Palomares, temporary water shutdowns occur frequently. Outages can last anywhere from 1 to 12 hours. In most cases, you simply have to wait for the supply to be restored.

CRITICAL WARNING FOR ELECTRIC BOILERS (WATER HEATERS):

  • Never draw hot water during a shutdown: Using hot water while the main supply is cut off can drain your electric boiler. This can lead to dry-firing and critical overheating of the internal elements.
  • Preemptive Action: To eliminate all risks, it is highly recommended to completely unplug or isolate the boiler from the power grid as soon as you notice a water cut.
  • Boiler stopped working? Do not replace it yet: If the boiler fails to heat up after water is restored but shows no physical leaks, never agree to have the entire unit replaced straight away. In most cases, the thermal safety switch has tripped. There is a red safety reset button located behind the bottom cover plate.
  • DANGER - 230V Electrical Hazard: The reset button is located next to live electrical terminals. Never attempt to open this cover yourself. Ensure a qualified person or certified electrician handles this. In case of issues, contact our local plumber via WhatsApp (Spanish/English) at +34 666 70 19 80 (closed on weekends).
  • Official Disruptions & Emergency Repairs: Planned service outages and emergency repair notices are published directly on Galasa's dedicated supply updates page: www.galasa.es/interrupciones-suministro.
