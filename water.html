Emergency Procedure: In Case of a Water Leak

If you detect or suspect a water leak, immediately follow these instructions based on the location:

A) Leak INSIDE the Building Infrastructure

In case of an internal building leak, the main water supply must be shut down immediately:

  1. Go to the main water cabinet located at the entrance of the underground garage, on the left side.
  2. The cabinet is locked. For rapid emergency response, the key can be accessed immediately via the key safe using the master access code.
  3. Once open, locate and completely close the main blue shut-off valve inside the cabinet to stop the primary water flow. (See reference photos below from the major 2025 leak event).

B) Leak OUTSIDE the Building

If a water leak is observed on the street, sidewalk, or main supply line outside the building perimeter:

  • Do not attempt to operate internal valves. Immediately report the issue to the Galasa emergency line at +34 950 39 31 11 and alert the building management.

C) Procedure in Case of a Main Water Shutdown (Water Cuts)

Due to the ageing main water supply infrastructure in Palomares, temporary water shutdowns occur frequently. Outages can last anywhere from 1 to 12 hours. In most cases, you simply have to wait for the supply to be restored.

CRITICAL WARNING FOR ELECTRIC BOILERS (WATER HEATERS):

  • Never draw hot water during a shutdown: Using hot water while the main supply is cut off can drain your electric boiler. This can lead to dry-firing and critical overheating of the internal elements.
  • Preemptive Action: To eliminate all risks, it is highly recommended to completely unplug or isolate the boiler from the power grid as soon as you notice a water cut.
  • Boiler stopped working? Do not replace it yet: If the boiler fails to heat up after water is restored but shows no physical leaks, never agree to have the entire unit replaced straight away. In most cases, the thermal safety switch has tripped. There is a red safety reset button located behind the bottom cover plate.
  • DANGER - 230V Electrical Hazard: The reset button is located next to live electrical terminals. Never attempt to open this cover yourself. Ensure a qualified person or certified electrician handles this. In case of issues, contact a professional plumber.
  • Official Announcements: Planned service disruptions and emergency repairs are published directly by Galasa on their website under www.galasa.es.
